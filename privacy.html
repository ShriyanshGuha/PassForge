Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PassForge. PassForge is a password generation and password strength analysis tool designed to help users create stronger and more memorable passwords.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information PassForge collects, how information is used, and how the website handles password-related data.

2. Passwords and Generated Data

PassForge currently performs password generation and password strength analysis directly in your web browser using JavaScript.

Passwords entered into the password field and passwords generated by PassForge are not intentionally sent to a PassForge server or stored in a PassForge database by the current version of the website.

Because PassForge operates on the client side, users should still avoid entering passwords that are currently used for important accounts into websites or applications that they do not trust.

3. Information We Collect

The current version of PassForge does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, phone numbers, or passwords through the password generator itself.

However, third-party services used by the website in the future, including advertising, analytics, hosting, security, or other services, may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies and Advertising

PassForge may use cookies or similar technologies in connection with third-party services such as advertising or analytics.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on PassForge, those services may use cookies to display and measure advertisements and may use information about users' interactions with websites and advertisements.

Additional information about Google's advertising practices and available privacy controls can be found in Google's own policies and settings.

5. Third-Party Services

PassForge may use third-party services to provide functionality such as hosting, analytics, advertising, security, or other website services.

These third parties may process certain technical information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Security

PassForge is designed so that password generation and analysis take place locally in the browser.

However, no website or computer system can guarantee absolute security. Users should always follow good password-security practices and use unique passwords for important accounts.

7. Children's Privacy

PassForge is not specifically directed toward children. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the password generator.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as PassForge develops or as additional services are introduced.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PassForge's privacy practices, please contact us through the Contact page.
